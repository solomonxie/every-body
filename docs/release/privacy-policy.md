# Privacy Policy — Every Body

_Last updated: 2026-10-02_

Every Body does not collect, transmit, or store your personal data. We operate no server, and the app makes no network requests.

## What the app stores
Only your language choice (English or 中文), kept on your device. Every other option — the figure you pick, view settings, recent searches — resets when the app closes.

## What leaves your device
Nothing. All anatomy models, charts, illustrations and texts ship inside the app and work offline. The source and licence links in Credits & licenses open in Safari only when you tap them.

## Permissions
None. The app does not use the camera, photos, microphone, location, contacts, Health data or motion sensors.

## Analytics and advertising
None. No analytics SDK, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers, no tracking across apps or websites.

## Health information
The app shows general educational content. It asks for no health information about you and stores none.

## Children
The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Deleting your data
Deleting the app removes the saved language choice.

## Changes
Material changes to this policy will be published here with a new date.
